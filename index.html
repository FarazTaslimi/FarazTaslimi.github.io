03 — About

Self-taught, vision-first

I'm Faraz — a self-taught frontend developer from Iran. For the past year and a half I've been learning on my own: no bootcamp, no shortcuts — just HTML, CSS and JavaScript, one honest concept at a time.

I care about vision before code. I start from how a page should feel, design it, then rebuild it by hand — which is why my work doesn't look like templates. Vanilla-first is a personal rule: if I can't explain what every line does, it doesn't ship.

I'm looking for my first real project — a full website, a rebuild, a collaboration — where a fresh pair of eyes with high standards can out-work a shelf of frameworks.
